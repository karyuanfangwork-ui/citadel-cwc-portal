BR-01 Customer Naming Rules

Rationale: Full legal names ensure accurate KYC verification, prevent duplicate records, and satisfy BNM regulatory requirements for customer identification. Abbreviations and nicknames break system matching and cause KYC failures.
Rules
Record TypeRuleEnforcement
AccountMust use the full registered company name exactly as per SSM / statutory documentsMin 5 chars
ContactMust use full name as per IC or Passport — no initials-only entriesPolicy
LeadIndividual: full name per IC. Corporate: registered company nameMin 5 chars
All RecordsNo placeholder text: "Test", "N/A", "TBC", "Unknown"Weekly audit
Examples
Good (Account): "Citadel Group Technologies Sdn Bhd"
Good (Contact): "Ahmad bin Abdullah"
Good (Lead): "Tan Mei Ling" or "ABC Holdings Sdn Bhd"
Bad (Account): "Citadel", "CGT", "CG Tech"
Bad (Contact): "Ahmad", "Mr. A", "TBC"
Bad (Lead): "Test Customer", "New Lead 1"
Risk: Incorrect naming causes KYC verification failure, creates duplicate records, and can result in BNM compliance findings during audit.
IC / Passport Number Format

Malaysian IC: 12 digits, no dashes or spaces (e.g., 901231045678). Passport: alphanumeric as printed. Enter consistently — system uses this for exact-match duplicate detection.

Phone Number Format

Malaysian numbers: include country code +60 (e.g., +60123456789). International: full international format. Do not use dashes, spaces, or brackets inconsistently.

BR-02 Lead Classification Rules

Rationale: Consistent lead classification ensures sales reps prioritise time on prospects with the highest conversion probability. Misclassification inflates the pipeline and wastes rep capacity on dead-end leads.
ClassificationCriteria (ALL must be met)AI Score RangeRep Action Required
🔥 Hot Responded within 48h AND expressed clear interest AND has identified budget AND decision-maker contacted 70 – 100 Follow up within same business day; prioritise above all Warm/Cold leads
🌡 Warm Responded but timeline or budget unclear OR interest expressed but not yet qualified 40 – 69 Follow up within 3 business days; nurture with relevant content
❄ Cold No response after 3 contact attempts OR explicitly expressed no current need 0 – 39 Schedule low-frequency nurture (monthly); do not invest heavy rep time
✗ Disqualified Cannot buy (budget), outside serviceable area, duplicate, or fraud suspicion N/A Mark Disqualified with reason; do not delete the record
AI Score Override Rule

If a rep's manual classification differs from the AI score by more than 20 points, the system flags the discrepancy. The rep may keep the manual classification but must add a justification note. Manager sign-off required for overrides on leads with AI score > 70.

Operational Scenario
Lead met at event, responded same day, confirmed budget of MYR 300k, CEO contact made → Hot. Priority follow-up today.
Lead received brochure 3 weeks ago, no response to 3 calls and 2 emails → Still marked Hot. Incorrect — should be Cold.

BR-07 Approval Rules

Rationale: Approval rules protect Citadel Group from unauthorised financial commitments and ensure all exceptions to standard terms are reviewed by the appropriate authority. No deal commitment may be made to a customer before the required approval is obtained.
Approval Authority Matrix
ScenarioThresholdMinimum Approver
Price discount1% – 5%Sales Manager
Price discount6% – 15%Senior Sales Manager
Price discount> 15%Senior Manager + Finance Director
Deal value> MYR 500,000Director
New account with credit facilityAnyCompliance + Sales Manager
Term extension / commercial exceptionAnySales Manager
Proposal re-submission after rejectionAnyOriginal approver
Non-Negotiable Rules
Self-Approval Prohibition

The submitter cannot be the approver. System enforces this — the approval form will not allow submission if submitter = approver. No exceptions.

Document Requirement

At least one supporting document must be attached. A justification note is mandatory. Submissions without these are rejected by the system before reaching the approver.

No Verbal Pre-Approvals

Verbal approvals from managers are not valid. All approvals must be recorded in the CRM approval system to be enforceable and auditable. No exceptions.

Good: Rep identifies 8% discount needed → submits approval request in CRM with proposal and justification → Senior Manager approves in system → Rep communicates to customer only after system approval received.
Bad: Rep verbally tells customer "my manager said it's fine" before submitting approval request. This is a policy violation — verbal approval is not binding and creates financial exposure.

BR-08 Access Control Rules

Rationale: Role-based access control (RBAC) ensures users can only see and modify records appropriate to their role. This protects customer data, maintains pipeline integrity, and satisfies PDPA and BNM data governance requirements.

Sales Manager crm:admin within own team; full read across team records Cannot delete Accounts without Admin approval; cannot modify system settings; cannot access other teams' records
Customer Service Read on Accounts + Contacts; write on Activities (complaint type) Cannot create/modify Leads or Opportunities; read-only on Pipeline; no Reports access
Operations Read on Accounts + Contacts; write on Trust Product records Cannot modify Leads/Opportunities; read-only Reports
Admin Full CRM access including bulk operations and user management Cannot approve financial deals (conflict of interest); all actions are audit-logged
Management Read-only on all records + full Reports access Cannot create/edit any CRM records; cannot modify system settings
Access Request Process

All access changes must be requested by the user's line manager — users cannot self-request access elevation. CRM Admin processes within 1 business day. All access grants are logged.

Access Revocation

Access must be revoked on the last day of employment. Manager must notify CRM Admin before the employee's final day. HR notifies Admin simultaneously. Non-compliance is a PDPA violation.

BR-10 Duplicate Prevention Rules

Rationale: Duplicate records fragment the customer view, produce inaccurate pipeline counts, and cause reps to contact the same customer twice from different records — a serious customer experience failure.
Prevention Rules
RuleDetail
Search-first mandateAll users must search before creating any new Lead, Account, or Contact record. Non-negotiable.
System detectionSystem flags: exact IC match, exact phone match, name similarity ≥ 85%
Duplicate warning acknowledgementWhen a warning is shown, user must review both records before proceeding. Cannot dismiss without reading.
Merge authorityOnly CRM Admin can execute a record merge. Users request via Admin; cannot self-merge.
Merge audit trailAll merge operations are permanently logged — which records merged, by whom, and when.
Duplicate rate targetTotal duplicate records must remain below 2% of all CRM records at all times.
Duplicate Detection Logic
When System Fires a Warning
  • Name similarity ≥ 85% (fuzzy match)
  • Exact IC / Passport number match
  • Exact phone number match (normalised format)
Decision Tree on Warning
  • Same person/company: Do not save new record. Update the existing record instead.
  • Different person, similar name: Save new record. Add a distinguishing note (e.g., IC number).
  • Uncertain: Tag as "Potential Duplicate" and notify CRM Admin for review within 48h.
Penalty: Creating 3+ duplicate records in a single week triggers a data quality flag for that user in the weekly report, reviewed by the Sales Manager.
Duplicate Detection & Merge UI
The system now provides a dedicated Duplicate Detection page at /crm/duplicates. This page lists all detected duplicate pairs, grouped by confidence level, and provides actions for merge or dismiss directly from the UI.
Confidence Scoring
Match LevelCriteriaConfidence RangeRequired Action
Exact MatchSame IC / Passport number95 – 100%Merge required
Strong MatchName + Phone match80 – 94%Review recommended
Possible MatchPartial name match60 – 79%Review optional
Merge Process
  • Primary selection: User selects which record becomes the primary (surviving) record. The secondary record is absorbed.
  • Field conflict resolution: Where both records have data in the same field, the primary record's value takes precedence. User may manually override individual fields.
  • Audit history: All merged fields and the secondary record ID are preserved in the audit log. The merge is reversible by Admin within 30 days.
Dismiss Action
  • Mark as not duplicate: User can dismiss a detected pair as "Not a Duplicate" with a required reason (e.g., "Different person — same company").
  • Dismissal logged: Every dismissal is permanently recorded with user, timestamp, and reason. Dismissed pairs are excluded from future detection runs unless re-activated by Admin.
