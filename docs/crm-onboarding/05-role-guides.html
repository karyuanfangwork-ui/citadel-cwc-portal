Role-Based User Guides — Overview

This guide defines what each CRM role can do, what they are responsible for, and what they cannot do. Find your role below and use it as your primary operational reference.
RolePrimary FunctionKey ModulesApproval AuthorityTeam Visibility
Sales Representative Prospect, qualify, close deals Dashboard, Leads, Accounts, Contacts, Pipeline None — submits requests only Own records only
Sales Manager Lead team, approve deals, drive pipeline All modules including Team Dashboard Discounts up to 15%, lead reassignment Full team visibility
Customer Service Handle complaints, resolve customer issues Dashboard, Accounts, Contacts, Activities None Own activities only
Operations Team Trust product setup, document compliance Accounts, Contacts, Reports (read) None Read-only on all accounts
Admin User Data governance, user management, system admin All modules + Admin Console Data corrections, access grants Full system access
Management Oversight, reporting, strategic decisions Dashboard, Reports, Team (read-only) Deals > MYR 500k, strategic exceptions Full read-only on all
Not sure which role applies to you? Check your permission level with your line manager or CRM Admin. Your accessible modules are determined by your assigned role — if you cannot see a module you need, submit an access request through your manager.

Individual Role Guides

Sales Representative

Front-line revenue generator. Owns the full customer journey from lead capture to deal close. The CRM is your primary work tool — every customer interaction lives here.

Permission Level
crm:read + crm:write
Record Visibility
Own assigned records only
Approval Authority
None — submits requests only
  Accessible Modules
Dashboard Accounts Contacts Leads Pipeline Reports Guide Team (hidden)
  Daily Responsibilities
1
Review AI Daily Briefing on Dashboard — identify priority customers to contact today
2
Clear all overdue tasks from the Task Panel before starting new outreach
3
Follow up with Hot leads — same-day response required
4
Update opportunity stages after every customer interaction (within 48h)
5
Log all activities within 24 hours of occurrence
6
Schedule the next follow-up before closing any completed activity
7
Search before creating any new record — prevent duplicates
  KPIs You Own
KPITarget
Lead Response Time< 24 hours
Activity Completion Rate> 85%
Personal Pipeline ValuePer quarterly target
Win Rate (personal)> 40%
Follow-Up SLA Compliance> 90%
Data Completeness (own records)> 95%
  Restrictions
  • Cannot view other sales reps' leads or opportunities
  • Cannot access the Team Dashboard tab
  • Cannot delete any CRM records
  • Cannot export reports (read-only on Reports module)
  • Cannot approve any discount or deal exception
  • Cannot modify system settings or user permissions
  Operational Dependencies

Depends on Sales Manager for lead assignment, approval decisions, and escalation support. Depends on CRM Admin for data corrections and access issues.

Sales Manager

Leads the sales team, drives pipeline performance, approves deals, and ensures SLA compliance across all reps. The Team Dashboard is your primary management tool.

Permission Level
crm:admin (own team)
Record Visibility
Full team — all leads, opportunities, accounts
Approval Authority
Discounts up to 15%; lead reassignment; exceptions
  Accessible Modules
Dashboard Accounts Contacts Leads Pipeline Team Dashboard Reports
  Daily Responsibilities
1
Review Team Dashboard — check unassigned leads, stale opportunities, and rep activity rates
2
Assign all unassigned leads within 4 business hours of creation
3
Review and respond to all pending approvals (SLA: 1 business day)
4
Coach any rep with 5+ overdue activities — triggered by system alert
5
Review weekly pipeline report every Monday 9am
6
Escalate unresolved customer complaints before SLA breach
7
Review AI Manager Intelligence on Team Dashboard for rep performance insights
  KPIs You Own
KPITarget
Team Lead Conversion Rate> 30%
Team Pipeline ValuePer quarterly target
Lead Assignment Rate100% within 4h
SLA Compliance Rate (team)> 90%
Approval Cycle Time< 24 hours
Team Win Rate> 40%
Rep Activity Completion Rate> 85%
  Restrictions
  • Cannot delete Account records without CRM Admin approval
  • Cannot approve discounts > 15% (requires Senior Manager)
  • Cannot approve deals > MYR 500,000 (requires Director)
  • Cannot self-approve any request submitted by themselves
  • Cannot modify system settings or user permissions
  • Cannot access other teams' records

Customer Service

Handles all customer complaints, queries, and escalations. Uses CRM account and activity history to investigate and resolve issues within SLA. The customer's primary advocate inside the organisation.

Permission Level
Read (Accounts/Contacts) + Write (Activities)
Record Visibility
All Accounts + Contacts (read); own Activities (write)
Approval Authority
None
  Accessible Modules
Dashboard Accounts (read) Contacts (read) Activities (write) Leads (read-only) Pipeline (read-only) Team (hidden) Reports (hidden)
  Daily Responsibilities
1
Review open complaint activities — prioritise High severity items first
2
Acknowledge all new complaints within SLA (30 min High / 2h Standard)
3
Investigate using the customer's full Account and Activity history in CRM
4
Coordinate with the relevant Sales Rep for context on the customer relationship
5
Communicate resolution to customer within the SLA window
6
Log detailed resolution note — do not close complaints without full documentation
7
Escalate any complaint approaching the 2-day resolution SLA to CS Manager proactively
  KPIs You Own
KPITarget
Complaint Resolution Rate> 95%
Avg Resolution Time (standard)< 2 business days
Avg Resolution Time (high)< 4 hours
Acknowledge SLA Compliance100%
Complaint Log Completeness100% of complaints logged with full notes
  Restrictions
  • Cannot create or modify Lead or Opportunity records
  • Cannot edit Account or Contact records (read-only)
  • Cannot access the Pipeline in edit mode
  • Cannot access Reports module
  • Cannot delete any complaint activity records — BNM retention applies
BNM Note: All complaint activity logs are retained for 7 years. Never request deletion of a complaint record — this is a regulatory violation.

Operations Team

Responsible for trust product setup, KYC document management, beneficiary records, and post-sale account onboarding. Activated by Sales after a Closed Won deal.

Permission Level
Read (Accounts/Contacts) + Write (Trust Products)
Record Visibility
All Accounts + Contacts (read); Trust Products (write)
Approval Authority
None
  Accessible Modules
Dashboard Accounts (read + Trust) Contacts (read) Reports (read) Leads (hidden) Pipeline (read-only) Team (hidden)
  Daily Responsibilities
1
Review newly converted Accounts (Closed Won) — action trust product setup
2
Check KYC document checklist completeness on active accounts — flag gaps to Sales Rep
3
Update beneficiary records when instructed by Sales or customer
4
Monitor trust product status lifecycle — update when milestones are reached
5
Alert Sales Rep and Manager when KYC documents are approaching expiry (30-day window)
6
Support sales reps on operational document tasks when requested
  KPIs You Own
KPITarget
Trust Product Onboarding Time< 5 business days from Closed Won
KYC Document Completeness100% of active accounts
Beneficiary Record Accuracy100% verified before trust activation
KYC Expiry Alert Lead Time30 days advance notice to Sales
  Restrictions
  • Cannot create or edit Lead or Opportunity records
  • Cannot move pipeline stages
  • Cannot access Reports in edit/export mode
  • Cannot approve any commercial terms
  • Cannot access Team Dashboard

Admin User

Owns CRM data quality, user access management, and system governance. Every admin action is permanently audit-logged. The Admin is the last line of data integrity defence.

Permission Level
Full CRM + Admin Console
Record Visibility
All records across all teams
Approval Authority
Data corrections, access grants, bulk operations
  Accessible Modules
Admin Console Dashboard Accounts Contacts Leads Pipeline Team Reports
  Daily / Weekly Responsibilities
1
Review data quality alerts and flagged records each morning
2
Process and resolve duplicate merge requests within 3 business days
3
Manage user access grants and revocations — same-day for leavers
4
Run weekly data quality audit every Monday — publish score report
5
Archive all exported reports per the 7-year retention policy
6
Monitor system notifications for anomalies and permission escalations
7
Quarterly: review user access rights against current org structure
  KPIs You Own
KPITarget
Data Completeness Rate> 95%
Duplicate Rate< 2%
KYC Compliance Rate100%
Access Request Turnaround< 1 business day
Leaver Access RevocationSame day as last day of employment
Weekly Quality Report On-Time100% (every Monday)
  Restrictions
  • Cannot approve financial deals or commercial discounts (conflict of interest)
  • All Admin actions are permanently audit-logged — no exceptions
  • Cannot grant own-account elevated permissions (must be authorised by Senior Manager)
  • Cannot delete complaint activity records

Management

Strategic oversight role. Uses CRM for read-only visibility into pipeline health, team performance, and forecast accuracy. Approves high-value deals and strategic exceptions.

Permission Level
Read-only (all records) + Reports
Record Visibility
Full read-only across all teams and records
Approval Authority
Deals > MYR 500k; strategic exceptions; Director-level
  Accessible Modules
Dashboard Team (read-only) Reports (full access) Create/Edit Records (blocked)
  Regular Responsibilities
1
Review executive dashboard KPIs at start of week — pipeline value, win rate, SLA compliance
2
Review weekly pipeline forecast report every Monday
3
Review monthly performance report by the 5th of each month
4
Attend weekly management review meeting — present pipeline findings
5
Approve Director-level deals (> MYR 500,000) within 2 business days
6
Review and set quarterly KPI targets with Sales Manager
  KPIs You Monitor
KPITarget
Total Pipeline Value> 3× monthly revenue target
Revenue Forecast Accuracy> 85%
Team Win Rate> 40%
Customer Retention Rate> 85% (quarterly)
Average Deal SizeMYR 250k+
KYC Compliance Rate100%
  Restrictions
  • Cannot create or edit any CRM records (read-only)
  • Cannot modify system settings or user permissions
  • Cannot share report exports externally without own sign-off documented
  • Cannot access Admin Console
Reporting Note: Dashboard KPI cards cache every 1 hour. For the most current figures before a board meeting, use the Reports module with real-time filters rather than the dashboard cards.
