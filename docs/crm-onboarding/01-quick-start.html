1. System Introduction

Welcome to CWC CRM. This guide is your starting point for using the Customer Relationship Management module. Read it fully before your first login.
  CRM Purpose

Manage the full customer lifecycle — from lead capture through to deal closure and post-sale trust product management — within a single platform aligned to Malaysian financial services compliance (BNM / KYC / AML).

  Business Objectives
  • Centralise all customer and prospect data
  • Reduce lead response time to under 24 hours
  • Improve pipeline forecast accuracy
  • Ensure BNM-compliant KYC documentation
  • Increase sales team productivity via AI assistance

2. Login Guide

Login Process
1
Open your browser — Chrome 110+, Edge 110+, or Firefox 115+. Safari 16+ is supported. Internet Explorer is not supported.
2
Navigate to the CWC portal URL provided by your IT administrator during onboarding.
3
Enter your corporate email address — use your Citadel Group email (@citadelgroup.com.my).
4
Enter your password — minimum 8 characters, case-sensitive. Do not share your password with colleagues.
5
Click "Sign In" — you will be directed to your role-appropriate dashboard.
  Session Timeout Policy: Sessions automatically expire after 8 hours of inactivity. Save any in-progress work before stepping away from your workstation.
  Account Lockout: After 5 consecutive failed login attempts, your account will be locked for 15 minutes. Contact IT Helpdesk if locked out.

5. Daily Core Activities

5.1 Create a New Lead
Sales Rep Manager

Purpose: Capture a new prospect into the CRM immediately upon contact. Every prospect must be in the system before any work is done on them.

Required Fields
FieldRequired?Guidance
Lead Name✱ RequiredFull legal name (individual) or registered company name — no abbreviations
Source✱ RequiredReferral / Cold Call / Website / Event / Walk-in — must not be blank
Phone or Email✱ RequiredAt least one valid contact method is mandatory
Assigned To✱ RequiredDefaults to yourself; managers can reassign after creation
Lead Value (Est.)OptionalEstimated deal value in MYR — enter if known at point of capture
NotesOptionalFirst contact context, referral source details, background info
  Common Mistake: Leaving Source blank. Every lead must have a traceable source for accurate pipeline and marketing reporting. Blank-source leads are flagged in the weekly data quality audit.
Step-by-Step
1
Search first — use global search to confirm no existing record for this person/company.
2
Click Leads in the sidebar menu.
3
Click "+ New Lead" button (top right of the Leads list).
4
Fill all required fields. If a duplicate warning appears, review the existing record before saving.
5
Click "Save Lead".
6
Add an initial activity note summarising first contact within 24 hours.

5.2 Assign a Lead
Manager

Purpose: Ensure every lead is assigned to an active sales representative within 4 business hours of creation. Unassigned leads are invisible to the team and never actioned.

1
Go to Leads list, filter by Status = Unassigned.
2
Review the lead details — source, estimated value, territory.
3
Click the lead row to open it, then click "Assign".
4
Select the appropriate sales representative from the dropdown.
5
The system sends an automatic notification to the assigned rep.
6
Confirm assignment is reflected in the Team Dashboard.
SLA: Lead assignment must occur within 4 business hours of lead creation. After 4 hours with no assignment, an automated alert is sent to the manager.
Reassignment: When reassigning an existing lead, a Reason for Reassignment note is required. This is recorded in the audit log.
5.3 Update an Opportunity
Sales Rep Manager

Purpose: Opportunities must be updated whenever a meaningful customer interaction occurs. Stale opportunities (no update for 14+ days) trigger automated escalation alerts and distort pipeline forecasts.

Opportunity Stages
StageMeaningRequired Action
ProspectingInitial contact madeLog first meeting or call as an activity
QualificationNeeds confirmedUpdate estimated value; attach initial KYC docs
ProposalProposal sentAttach proposal document; set next follow-up date
NegotiationTerms being discussedLog each negotiation call; keep close date current
Closed WonDeal agreedConvert to Account/Contact; initiate trust product setup
Closed LostDeal lostMandatory: fill Lost Reason field
Steps to Update
1
Open Pipeline (Kanban) or Opportunities list view.
2
Click the opportunity card or row to open the detail page.
3
Click "Edit" or update fields inline on the Kanban card.
4
Update stage, estimated value, and projected close date.
5
Log a new activity note describing the interaction that prompted the update.
6
Click "Save".
Rule: Stages cannot be skipped. You cannot move directly from Prospecting to Negotiation.
5.4 Add a Customer Activity
Sales Rep Customer Service

Purpose: Log every customer interaction — call, email, meeting, site visit — within 24 hours of it occurring. Activities form the customer history that managers, AI, and auditors rely on.

1
Open the relevant Lead, Opportunity, Account, or Contact detail page.
2
Click "+ Add Activity" button in the Activities tab.
3
Select activity type: Call / Meeting / Email / Task / Note.
4
Fill the Subject and write a descriptive note covering what was discussed and any action items.
5
Set Date to when the activity actually occurred (not today's date if it happened earlier).
6
Click "Save Activity".
AI Tip: Use the AI Note Analysis feature — paste your meeting notes and the AI will extract action items, sentiment signals, and next steps automatically.
Rule: Activities logged more than 24 hours after occurrence are flagged as Late Entry in reporting. Log same-day whenever possible.
5.5 Schedule a Follow-Up
Sales Rep
1
Open any Lead, Opportunity, Account, or Contact detail page.
2
Click "+ Add Activity".
3
Set type to Call, Meeting, or Task.
4
Set a Due Date in the future — this is what populates your Dashboard task panel.
5
Write a brief note explaining the purpose of the follow-up.
6
Click "Save Activity". It appears in your Task Panel immediately.
  Best Practice: Never complete an activity without scheduling the next follow-up. "Zero-gap follow-up" — no period where a prospect has no scheduled next touch — is a tracked team KPI.
Automation: The system sends you an email reminder 24 hours before each scheduled follow-up activity. You can also see all upcoming activities in your Dashboard Task Panel.
5.6 Submit an Approval Request
Sales Rep Manager
1
Open the relevant opportunity or account record.
2
Click "Request Approval" in the action bar.
3
Select the approval type (Discount / Exception / High-Value Deal).
4
Attach all supporting documents (proposals, pricing sheets, KYC docs).
5
Write a clear justification note — required, cannot submit without it.
6
Click "Submit". Approver receives notification immediately.
7
Track status in the Approvals tab. Follow up if pending beyond 24 hours.
Approval Triggers: Discounts > 5% require Manager approval. Discounts > 15% require Senior Manager + Finance. Deals > MYR 500,000 require Director sign-off.
SLA: Approval requests must be responded to within 1 business day. If no response after 24 hours, the system auto-escalates to the next approver level.
5.7 Search a Customer
All Users
1
Click the Search bar in the top navigation, or press Ctrl+K (Windows) / ⌘K (Mac).
2
Type at least 3 characters — the name, IC number, company registration number, or phone number.
3
Results appear grouped by type: Accounts, Contacts, Leads, Opportunities.
4
Click the matching result to open the full detail record.
Tip: Search by IC number (e.g., 901231045678) for exact individual matches. This is the most reliable way to find a specific person and avoid accidental duplicate record creation.
Always search before creating. If a record already exists, add information to the existing record rather than creating a new one.
5.8 Review Your Dashboard (Daily Start-of-Day Routine)
All Users

Every working day should begin with a 5-minute dashboard review. This keeps you on top of priorities and ensures nothing falls through the cracks.

Step 1 — AI Briefing

Read the AI Daily Briefing. It highlights which customers need attention most urgently based on activity history and lead scores.

Step 2 — Overdue Tasks

Check the Task Panel for items in red (overdue). Action these before starting new work — overdue tasks impact your SLA compliance KPI.

Step 3 — Notifications

Open the Notifications centre. Clear new lead assignments, approval decisions, and system alerts before starting your prospect outreach.

Manager Management

6. Best Practices Guide

  Data Quality
Always search for an existing record before creating a new one — prevent duplicates from day one.
Use the full legal name for all Accounts and Contacts — no abbreviations or nicknames.
Enter IC / Passport numbers in the exact standard format — 12 digits, no dashes or spaces.
Update opportunity stage within 48 hours of any customer interaction.
Never leave the Source field blank on a lead record.
Do NOT enter placeholder values like "Test Customer", "N/A", or "TBC" in mandatory fields.
Do NOT create a new record if a duplicate warning appears — investigate first.
  Customer Handling
Respond to all customer enquiries within your SLA window — check your dashboard daily.
Log complaint activities with full details — these are reviewed in compliance audits.
Escalate unresolved issues before the SLA breach point — do not wait for the auto-escalation.
  Activity Logging
Log every customer interaction — call, email, site visit, online meeting — on the same day it occurs.
Use the AI Note Analysis feature to extract action items from long meeting notes automatically.
Use the correct activity type (Call / Meeting / Email) — not generic "Task" for everything.
Schedule the next follow-up before marking an activity as complete — zero-gap follow-up is mandatory.
  Approval Submission
Attach all supporting documents before submitting — an incomplete submission will be rejected.
Write a clear, specific justification note — vague notes cause delays and rejections.
Check approval status within 24 hours — follow up with your manager if pending without response.
  Reporting Accuracy
Review report data for anomalies before presenting — investigate outliers rather than presenting raw data.
Archive all exported reports per the Citadel data retention policy (minimum 7 years).
Do NOT share report exports externally without explicit management authorisation.
