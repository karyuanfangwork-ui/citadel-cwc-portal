Duplicate Detection & Merge

Duplicate records fragment the customer view and cause wasted effort — multiple reps may contact the same customer, pipeline data becomes unreliable, and compliance reporting is skewed. The duplicate detection engine runs automatically on every record creation and update.
Duplicate Detection Flow

Merge Process
  1. Select Primary Record — the record that will survive as the canonical entry
  2. Field-by-Field Conflict Resolution — for each conflicting field, choose which value to keep
  3. Merged Record Preserves — all activities, notes, and audit history from both records are retained on the primary
  4. Secondary Record Soft-Deleted — the duplicate is marked inactive; it is not permanently removed for audit purposes
Dismiss a Duplicate Alert

If a user determines the match is incorrect, they may mark it as "not a duplicate" by providing a reason. The dismissal is permanently logged in the audit trail for compliance review.

Duplicate Prevention Rules
IC-Based Matching

IC/Passport number is the primary unique identifier. Two records with the same IC are always flagged as an exact match.

Name Normalization

Names are normalised (case-insensitive, whitespace collapsed, common aliases) before matching to catch minor spelling differences.

Phone Format Normalization

Phone numbers are stripped of formatting characters and country-code normalised before comparison to detect matches across different entry formats.

Good: System flags two accounts with same IC number 901231045678 → Rep reviews → selects primary account → merges contacts and activities.
Bad: Rep creates a second account for an existing customer because "I couldn't find the first one" → Never searched before creating.

Admin Configuration
CrmAnomalyConfig Model

CRM Admin configures anomaly detection via the CrmAnomalyConfig model. For each anomaly type, the admin can:

  • Set custom thresholds (e.g., change Stale Opportunity from 14 to 21 days)
  • Enable or disable individual anomaly types
  • Adjust severity levels (High / Medium / Low)
Alert Cards UI
CrmAnomalyCards Component

The CrmAnomalyCards component provides the user interface for managing anomaly alerts:

  • Accessible at /crm/anomalies — list of all active alerts
  • Filter by type, severity, and status (Open / Resolved / Dismissed)
  • Bulk dismiss capability for low-severity alerts after review
System auto-generates alerts Policy: Manager review within 24h
Policy: Managers are required to review anomaly alerts within 24 hours of generation. Unreviewed alerts are escalated to the next-level manager after 24 hours.

AI Score Persistence
Lead AI Scoring

Three fields on the Lead model persist AI-generated scores:

  • aiScore — numeric score (0–100)
  • aiScoreReason — text explanation of the score
  • aiScoredAt — timestamp of last AI scoring
Opportunity Win Probability

Two fields on the Opportunity model persist AI-generated win predictions:

  • aiWinProbability — percentage (0–100%)
  • aiWinReason — text explanation of the prediction
Important: AI scores are indicators, not decisions. Managers may override AI recommendations with written justification. AI models are retrained quarterly using the latest CRM data. Scores should inform judgment — not replace it.

Audit Trail

Every significant CRM action is permanently logged in the audit trail, accessible per entity. The audit trail provides a complete, tamper-proof history of who did what and when — critical for regulatory compliance and dispute resolution.
CrmAuditLog.tsx Component
Timeline View

The CrmAuditLog.tsx component is accessible on every entity detail page. It renders a timeline of changes in reverse chronological order, showing the user, action, timestamp, and field-level details for each event.

Access
API Endpoint

GET /audit/:entityType/:entityId — Any user with read access to the entity can view its audit trail. Access is controlled by the same role-based permissions that govern the entity itself.

BNM Retention Requirement

Audit logs are retained for a minimum of 7 years, consistent with BNM record-keeping requirements for complaint records and financial transaction documentation. After 7 years, logs may be archived to cold storage but cannot be purged without regulatory approval.

Warning: Audit logs cannot be deleted or modified, even by CRM Admin. This is a regulatory requirement. Any attempt to tamper with audit logs would constitute a compliance breach and must be reported to the Compliance team immediately.

PDPA & Consent

Marketing Opt-In RequiredPolicyMarketing emails may only be sent to contacts with marketingOptIn=true. This is a separate consent from PDPA consent.

Good: Rep explains data usage to new prospect, obtains verbal consent, records pdpaConsent=true with today's date.
Bad: Rep sends marketing email to Contact with pdpaConsent=false → PDPA violation.
PDPA vs. BNM: How They Relate

PDPA is Malaysian federal law governing personal data handling across all sectors. BNM guidelines are sector-specific regulations for financial institutions. Both apply simultaneously. PDPA governs how personal data is collected, used, and disclosed. BNM guidelines govern trust business practices, including KYC, complaint handling, and record retention. Compliance with one does not substitute for the other.

CRM-Credit Bridge

BorrowerProfile links CRM records to the Credit module, enabling a seamless handoff from sales to credit assessment. Once an opportunity reaches Closed Won, the linked BorrowerProfile ensures credit assessment has accurate, verified data.

Key Responsibilities
Operations Team

Must verify the BorrowerProfile link is established after an opportunity reaches Closed Won. Unlinked records block credit assessment initiation.

One-Way Sync Only

There is no write-back from Credit to CRM currently. All data flows one-way from CRM into the Credit module. Credit-side changes do not update CRM records.

Permission Note: Access to the Credit module requires separate permissions (credit:read / credit:write). CRM permissions do not automatically grant Credit module access. Contact your system administrator to request access.
