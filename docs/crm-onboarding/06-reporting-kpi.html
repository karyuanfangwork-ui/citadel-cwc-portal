KPI Definitions

All 18 CRM KPIs are defined below with formula, target, owner, and data source. KPI targets are reviewed and updated quarterly by Sales Manager and Management.

KPI Name Formula Target Frequency Owner Data Source Business Value
Lead Response Time Avg hours from lead creation to first activity logged < 24h Daily Sales Manager Lead Activity Report Slower response = lower conversion. Industry benchmark: contact within 1h is 7× more likely to qualify.
Lead Conversion Rate (Qualified Leads ÷ Total Leads) × 100 > 30% Weekly Sales Manager Lead Conversion Report Measures qualification efficiency. Low rate signals poor lead quality or rep skill gap.
Lead Assignment Rate (Assigned Leads ÷ Total Leads) × 100 100% Daily Sales Manager Leads List Unassigned leads receive zero outreach. Rate below 100% means direct revenue leakage.
Opportunity Win Rate (Closed Won ÷ Total Closed) × 100 > 40% Monthly Sales Manager Pipeline Report Core sales effectiveness measure. Tracks deals won vs. total decisions made.
Average Deal Size Total Closed Won Value ÷ Number of Won Deals MYR 250k+ Monthly Management Sales Performance Report Tracks whether reps are closing appropriate-value deals. Decline signals downmarket drift.
Pipeline Coverage Total Active Pipeline Value ÷ Monthly Revenue Target > 3× Weekly Management Pipeline Forecast Report Ensures enough pipeline exists to meet targets even with typical attrition. Below 3× is a risk signal.
Average Sales Cycle Avg days from Prospecting to Closed Won < 90 days Monthly Management Pipeline Report Longer cycles tie up rep capacity and delay revenue. Elongation signals negotiation or qualification issues.
Activity Completion Rate (Completed Activities ÷ Scheduled Activities) × 100 > 85% Weekly Sales Manager Activity Report Measures rep follow-through. Low rate indicates overloading or poor time management.
Follow-Up SLA Compliance (On-time Activities ÷ Total Due Activities) × 100 > 90% Weekly Sales Manager Activity Report Ensures customers are touched within the committed timeframe. SLA breach correlates with deal loss.
Complaint Resolution Rate (Resolved Complaints ÷ Total Complaints) × 100 > 95% Weekly CS Manager Activity Report (Complaint type) BNM-sensitive. Unresolved complaints create regulatory exposure and customer churn.
Avg Complaint Resolution Time Avg hours from complaint log to resolved status < 48h Weekly CS Manager Activity Report Direct driver of customer satisfaction. High resolution time triggers BNM scrutiny.
Approval Cycle Time Avg hours from approval submission to decision < 24h Weekly Sales Manager Approval module Long approval cycles delay deal closure and frustrate customers mid-negotiation.
Data Completeness Rate (Records with all mandatory fields ÷ Total Records) × 100 > 95% Weekly CRM Admin Data Quality Report Incomplete records produce unreliable reports and fail KYC checks.
Duplicate Rate (Duplicate Records ÷ Total Records) × 100 < 2% Weekly CRM Admin Data Quality Report Duplicates fragment customer view and cause reps to contact the same prospect twice.
KYC Compliance Rate (Accounts with complete KYC docs ÷ Active Accounts) × 100 100% Monthly CRM Admin / Compliance KYC Compliance Report BNM mandatory. Any active account without complete KYC is a regulatory violation.
Customer Retention Rate ((Customers End − New Customers) ÷ Customers Start) × 100 > 85% Quarterly Management Customer Report Acquiring new customers costs 5× more than retaining existing ones. A core business health indicator.
Anomaly Resolution Rate (Resolved anomalies ÷ Total anomalies) × 100 > 90% Weekly CRM Admin Anomaly Report Unresolved anomalies represent undetected data quality or compliance risks.
Quota Attainment (Closed Won Value ÷ Quota Target) × 100 ≥ 90% Monthly Sales Manager Quota Attainment Report Below 90% signals pipeline insufficiency or rep underperformance.

Report Types

The CRM Reports module provides 9 structured report types. Each report supports date range filters, team/rep filters, and CSV export. Navigate to Reports in the CRM sidebar to access all reports.
Pipeline Report

Shows the current distribution of all active opportunities across pipeline stages, total value per stage, and a 90-day weighted revenue forecast based on AI win probability.

FrequencyWeekly — every Monday 9am (auto)
AudienceSales Team + Management
Key KPIsPipeline Coverage, Win Rate, Avg Deal Size
 CSV export available  ·   Filterable by rep, stage, date
Lead Conversion Report

Breaks down lead volume by source (Referral, Cold Call, Website, Event, Walk-in), shows conversion rates through the qualification funnel, and tracks average time-to-qualify per source.

FrequencyMonthly — 5th of each month
AudienceSales Manager + Management
Key KPIsLead Conversion Rate, Lead Response Time
 CSV export available  ·   Filterable by source, rep, date
Sales Performance Report

Rep-by-rep comparison of deals won, revenue generated, win rate, average deal size, and activity completion rate. Identifies top performers and underperformers for coaching.

FrequencyMonthly — 5th of each month
AudienceSales Manager + Management + Board
Key KPIsWin Rate, Avg Deal Size, Activity Rate
 CSV export available  ·   Filterable by rep, period
Activity Report

Shows activity volume by type (Call, Meeting, Email, Task), completion rates, overdue activities, and late-entry logging. Used to coach reps on follow-up discipline.

FrequencyWeekly — Monday 9am (auto)
AudienceSales Manager
Key KPIsActivity Completion Rate, Follow-Up SLA
 CSV export available  ·   Filterable by rep, type, date
KYC Compliance Report

Lists all active accounts with their KYC document completeness status, flags accounts with expiring or missing documents, and tracks KYC renewal timelines. BNM audit-ready.

FrequencyMonthly — 5th of each month
AudienceCompliance + Management
Key KPIsKYC Compliance Rate, Expiry Alerts
 CSV export available  ·   BNM retention: 7 years
Pipeline Forecast Report

Generates a 90-day weighted revenue forecast by multiplying each opportunity's estimated value by its AI win probability. Segmented by stage, rep, and industry. Used for board-level presentations.

FrequencyMonthly — 5th of each month (also on-demand)
AudienceBoard / Directors / Management
Key KPIsPipeline Coverage, Forecast Accuracy
 CSV export available  ·   Filterable by stage, rep, period
Win/Loss Analysis Report

Aggregates all Closed Lost opportunities by Lost Reason, stage at which they were lost, competitor involved (if known), and rep. Identifies systemic patterns for process improvement.

FrequencyQuarterly — end of quarter (also on-demand)
AudienceSales Manager + Management
Key KPIsWin Rate, Lost Reason Distribution
 CSV export available  ·  Requires Lost Reason field populated
Anomaly Report

Aggregates all active anomaly alerts generated by the AI detection engine. Shows anomaly type, severity, affected records, and resolution status. Used by managers and admins to monitor data quality and compliance risks.

FrequencyOn-demand — real-time from AI Alerts page
AudienceSales Manager + CRM Admin
Key KPIsAnomaly Resolution Time, Data Quality Score
 AI-generated  ·   See Data Governance Guide
Quota Attainment Report

Shows quarterly sales quota targets versus actual Closed Won revenue, segmented by rep, territory, and quota type (Revenue / Deal Count / New Accounts). Colour-coded attainment: green ≥90%, amber 70-89%, red below 70%.

FrequencyMonthly — 5th of each month
AudienceManagement + Sales Manager
Key KPIsQuota Attainment %, Pipeline Coverage
 CSV export available  ·  See Territory & Quota Guide
  How to Run Any Report
1 Click Reports in the CRM sidebar
2 Select report type from the dropdown
3 Set date range and filters (rep, stage, source)
4 Review for anomalies before sharing
5 Click Export CSV if archiving is required

Reporting Governance

  Never Do
  • Share CRM reports externally without management approval
  • Export data to personal devices (home laptop, personal email)
  • Present report data without reviewing for anomalies first
  • Screenshot pipeline data for sharing in unapproved channels
  Always Do
  • Review report data for anomalies before sharing
  • Archive CSV exports per the 7-year retention policy
  • Obtain management sign-off before any external report sharing
  • Use the Reports module (not dashboard cache) for live data
  System Controls
  • All CSV exports logged: timestamp + user identity + report type
  • Dashboard KPI cards cache for 1 hour
  • Scheduled reports auto-delivered to owners by email
  • KPI targets locked — only CRM Admin can update after quarterly review
