Section 02

Product Overview

The CRM module is a full-lifecycle customer relationship management system engineered for Malaysian financial services — specifically trust products, KYC/AML compliance, and long-cycle relationship selling.

Core CRM

Accounts, Contacts, Leads, Opportunities — complete company and individual record management with Malaysian-specific fields (MyKad, SSM registration, PDPA flags).

Pipeline Management

Visual Kanban board with drag-and-drop stage progression, configurable pipelines, win probability tracking, and stage history logging.

AI Intelligence

Lead scoring, win probability prediction, daily briefing, next-best-action recommendations, KYC gap detection, and compliance risk profiling.

Trust & Compliance

Trust product lifecycle (Living, Testamentary, Charitable, Cash), beneficiary management, BNM-aligned KYC workflows, PDPA consent tracking, and document checklist.

Reporting & Analytics

7 configurable report types: lead conversion, pipeline forecast, sales performance, activity summary, KYC compliance, stale leads, and win/loss analysis. CSV export on all.

Automation Engine

7 scheduled background jobs: activity reminders, lead aging alerts, stale deal escalation, KYC expiry warnings, rep inactivity tracking, and SLA monitoring.

1
Log in
Navigate to CRM → Dashboard. AI Daily Briefing generates automatically if not refreshed today.
2
Read AI Daily Briefing
Structured card shows: today's priority leads, overdue follow-ups, pipeline deals stalling, and key alerts.
3
Review KPI Hero Banner
Total leads, active opportunities, pipeline value, conversion rate — all personalised to your data.
4
Act on overdue items
Click any overdue lead/opportunity directly from the dashboard to open the detail page.
5
Log first activity
From the lead detail, click "+ Activity" to log a call, email, or meeting. Notes are AI-analysed for action items.
Dashboard Components
  • AI Daily Briefing card (top of page)
  • KPI Hero Banner (leads, opps, value, rate)
  • My Performance mini-chart
  • Recent Activities feed
  • Overdue Follow-ups alert list
  • Won/Lost This Month summary
Journey 2 — Account Management Rep / Manager
1
Create a new Account
CRM → Accounts → "+ New Account". Enter company name, SSM registration, industry, AUM, address.
2
Attach contacts
From the Account detail page, link existing contacts or create new ones inline.
3
Link opportunities
Open opportunities tab — see all active deals associated with this account.
4
Inline-edit account details
Click any field directly to edit — no modal required. Changes are saved and audit-logged immediately.
5
View audit trail
Activity History tab shows every change: who modified what field, when, and the previous value.
Journey 3 — Contact Management & KYC Rep / Compliance
1
Create contact
Enter name, MyKad/Passport number, DOB, nationality, and contact details. PDPA consent date recorded.
2
Check AI KYC Gap Detection

3
Complete document checklist
Tick off each document as received. Status auto-updates: PENDING → SUBMITTED → APPROVED.
4
Set risk profile
Assign LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH/VERY_HIGH risk classification. AI compliance risk narrative generated on request.
5
Convert qualified lead
When lead is QUALIFIED, click "Convert Lead" — creates Contact + Account + Opportunity in one atomic transaction.

1

3

4
Use AI Draft Message
Click "Draft Message" — AI generates personalised outreach email. Edit and send.
5
Qualify or convert
Update status to QUALIFIED, then click "Convert" to create the full CRM record set.

Close deal (WON / LOST)

Section 11

SOP / User Guide

Standard Operating Procedures for sales reps, managers, and compliance officers. Reference guide for onboarding and day-to-day operations.

SOP-001 — Sales Rep Daily Routine

Morning Routine (9:00–9:30 AM)

  1. Open CRM → Dashboard. Read AI Daily Briefing.
  2. Action any overdue follow-ups from the alert list.
  3. Check notifications: stale lead alerts, activity reminders.
  4. Review today's priority leads (top-3 by AI score).
  5. Log first outbound activity (call/email) before 10:00 AM.

Activity Logging Requirements

Mandatory: Log every client interaction within 24 hours. Unlogged interactions cannot be audited and count against activity KPI.

  • Calls: log within 2 hours, include call outcome
  • Emails: log as EMAIL activity with subject
  • Meetings: log notes within same day; use AI Note Analysis
  • Set follow-up date on every activity
SOP-002 — KYC Submission Procedure

Regulatory requirement: All contacts must have approved KYC before a trust product can be activated. BNM requires KYC renewal every 2 years.

  1. Create/open the Contact record. Navigate to the KYC tab.
  2. Click "Run AI KYC Check" to identify gaps.
  3. Collect all flagged documents: IC copy (front + back), utility bill (≤3 months), bank statement (≤3 months), source of funds declaration.
  4. Upload or tick each item on the document checklist.
  5. Set risk profile (LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH/VERY_HIGH) based on client profile.
  6. Change KYC status to SUBMITTED. Compliance officer is notified.
  7. Compliance officer reviews and sets status to APPROVED or returns with comments.
  8. On APPROVAL: trust product creation may proceed.
SOP-003 — Trust Product Onboarding

Pre-requisite: KYC must be APPROVED for the primary contact before proceeding. Opportunity must be in WON or NEGOTIATION stage.

  1. Open the opportunity. Navigate to Trust Products tab.
  2. Click "+ Create Trust Product". Select trust type.
  3. Enter trust value (RM), deed date, and trustee details.
  4. Add all beneficiaries. Verify percentage allocations total 100%.
  5. Complete document checklist: trust deed, IC copies for all beneficiaries, asset schedule, witness signatures, and source of funds (if not already in KYC).
  6. AI reviews document checklist for gaps. Resolve all flagged items.
  7. Change status to PENDING_REVIEW. Manager + Compliance are notified.
  8. After internal review and approval, status is set to ACTIVE.
  9. Schedule annual review reminder in Activities (follow-up date = deed date + 1 year).
SOP-004 — Manager Weekly Review
  1. Open CRM → Team Dashboard. Switch date range to "This Week".
  2. Review team leaderboard. Note reps below target activity count.
  3. Check "Rep Inactivity Alerts" — action any rep with 0 activities this week.
  4. Open Pipeline view. Filter by "Stale Deals" (14+ days, no movement).
  5. For each stale deal: review stage, contact rep, or reassign.
  6. Run AI Manager Briefing for the week's pipeline health summary.
  7. Run "Pipeline Forecast" report. Export CSV for weekly business review.
  8. Conduct 1:1 catch-up with any rep showing declining lead conversion rate.
