DOCUMENT 1

User Quick Start Guide

Purpose
Get a new user productive in under 10 minutes.
Scope
Login, navigation, first request, tracking.
Audience
All employees (especially new joiners).

Step 1 — Log in

  1. Open the CWC 2.0 portal in your browser. The Login screen appears.
  2. Enter your company email and password, then select Sign In.
  3. Forgot your password? Select Forgot password to reach the Forgot Password screen and follow the email link.

Step 2 — Get your bearings (the Dashboard)

After login you land on the Dashboard — your home base. From here you can see a summary of your requests, recent company announcements, and quick links to the three service desks.

WhereScreenUse it to…
Home/ DashboardSee request summary cards and announcements.
My Requests/my-requestsView, search and filter everything you've raised.
Approvals/approvals(Managers) Approve or reject items waiting on you.
Inbox/inboxRead in-app notifications and mark them read.
Knowledge Base/kbFind self-help articles before raising a ticket.

Step 3 — Raise your first request

  1. From the Dashboard, choose the desk you need: IT Support, HR Services or Group Finance.
  2. Pick the category that matches your need (e.g. Get IT Help).
  3. The Create Request wizard opens: Select Type → Fill Details → Review & Submit.
  4. Complete the required fields, attach any files, and select Submit.
  5. You'll get a confirmation and a request ID — your ticket is now Submitted.

Step 4 — Track it

Open My Requests and select your ticket to reach the Request Detail page. The Workflow Stepper at the top shows exactly where your request is and what happens next. Add comments, upload files, and respond when a request is marked Action Required.

Best Practices
  • Search the Knowledge Base first — many issues have an instant answer.
  • Pick the most specific category; it routes your ticket to the right team faster.
  • Write a clear, one-line summary and attach screenshots where relevant.
Common Mistakes
  • Raising an IT ticket under HR (or vice-versa) — this delays routing.
  • Leaving required fields vague (“not working”) instead of describing the symptom.
  • Closing the browser before pressing Submit on the final wizard step.
Troubleshooting
  • Can't log in? Use Forgot password; if it persists, raise a Get IT Help ticket via a colleague or call the IT desk.
  • Don't see a desk or category? Some categories are role-restricted (e.g. hiring). See Document 9.
Example Scenario Priya's laptop won't connect to Wi-Fi. She opens IT Support → Get IT Help, describes the symptom, attaches a screenshot of the error, and submits. Her ticket is Submitted, then In Review within the 24-hour SLA, and she's notified at each step.

End User Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Purpose
Define the official procedure for raising and managing a request end-to-end.
Scope
All three desks; creation through closure.
Audience
All employees, managers, approvers.

2.1 The Standard Request Lifecycle

Submit→ In Review→ Approval
(if required)→ In Progress→ Action
Required?→ Resolved / Completed

2.2 Procedure

  1. Identify the need and the right desk. Check the Service Catalog to choose the correct request type.
  2. Self-serve first. Search the Knowledge Base. Raise a ticket only if no article resolves it.
  3. Create the request via the Create Request wizard. Complete all required fields and attach supporting documents.
  4. Submit and record the ID. The system assigns a tracking ID and sets status to Submitted. You and the assigned team are notified.
  5. Respond promptly to “Action Required”. If the agent needs more information, the ticket moves to Action Required and the SLA may pause until you reply.
  6. Approvals (where applicable). Procurement, hiring and finance items route to approvers automatically. The SLA timer pauses while awaiting approval (see Business Rules).
  7. Confirm resolution. When the agent marks the ticket Resolved / Completed, review the outcome. Re-open by commenting if the issue persists.

2.3 User Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate, complete information and valid business justification.
  • Respond to Action Required requests within the stated window.
  • Use the correct desk and category; do not raise duplicate tickets.
  • Keep confidential information appropriate to the request.

2.4 Department Responsibilities

DepartmentOwnsResponsible for
IT SupportIT desk & assetsTriage, technical resolution, procurement of approved hardware/software, asset provisioning.
HR ServicesHR deskHR queries, recruitment workflow, onboarding & offboarding coordination.
Group FinanceFinance deskPurchase requisitions, chargebacks, budget review, payment processing.
Approvers / ManagersApproval decisionsTimely review of items routed to them; documented approve/reject decisions.
Best Practices One issue per ticket; reference related ticket IDs in comments rather than merging unrelated problems.
Common Mistakes Emailing an agent directly instead of commenting on the ticket — off-ticket conversations are not tracked and can break the SLA record.
Troubleshooting If a ticket appears “stuck”, open it and read the Workflow Stepper — it usually shows it's awaiting your action or an approver's decision.

DOCUMENT 4

Ticket Lifecycle Guide

Purpose
Explain every ticket status and how requests progress.
Scope
7 workflow tracks; the Request Detail & Workflow Stepper screens.
Audience
All employees and approvers.

CWC 2.0 uses different status tracks depending on the request type. Most simple tickets only ever use the General track below. Procurement, hiring, onboarding, offboarding, finance and chargeback requests follow their own specialised tracks. The Workflow Stepper on the Request Detail page always shows your ticket's position in plain language.

4.1 General Track (most tickets)

StatusMeaningWhat you should do
SubmittedTicket received, awaiting triage.Nothing — wait for the team.
In ReviewAn agent is assessing it.Be available for questions.
Action RequiredThe team needs more info from you.Respond — the SLA may be paused on you.
In ProgressActively being worked on.Wait; monitor for updates.
WaitingPaused on a dependency (e.g. third party).Wait; check comments for context.
ApprovedAn approval gate was passed.Nothing — it proceeds automatically.
ResolvedWork is done.Confirm; comment to re-open if needed.
CompletedClosed and finalised.None.
RejectedDeclined (with reason).Read the reason; raise a corrected request if appropriate.

4.2 IT Hardware & Procurement Track

Acknowledged→ CEO Approval→ CTO Approval→ CFO Approval→ Procurement→ Ordered→ Received→ Provisioned→ Resolved

Statuses you may see (friendly labels): CEO Approval CTO Approval CFO Approval Procurement In Progress Hardware Ordered Hardware Received Software Provisioned Payment Processing Pending Delivery. A rejection at any gate ends the request (e.g. CEO Rejected).

4.3 HR Hiring Track

Submitted→ CEO Approval→ Group CEO Approval→ Job Posted→ Manager Review→ Interview→ HR Screening→ LOA→ Completed

Includes Interview Scheduled, Interview Feedback Pending, HR Screening, and the Letter of Acceptance steps LOA Pending Approval → LOA Approved → LOA Issued → LOA Accepted. A rejected candidate (Candidate Rejected) loops back to Job Posted to resume recruitment.

4.4 Onboarding Track

Submitted→ HR Approval→ Pre-Arrival Setup→ Ready for Day 1→ Day 1 Orientation→ Week 1→ Month 1–3 Milestones→ Completed

4.5 Offboarding Track

Submitted→ Notice Period→ Knowledge Transfer→ Final Week→ Exit Procedures→ Completed

4.6 Finance Track (Purchase & Reimbursement)

Pending Ack→ Acknowledged→ In Progress→ Finance Head / CFO Approval→ Payment Processing→ Closed

You may see Pending Manager Approval, Pending Finance Head Approval, Pending CFO Approval, Payment Processing, Payment Completed, Reimbursement Closed. A rejection routes the request back to Submitted so you can correct and resubmit.

4.7 Inter-Company Chargeback Track

From-Entity Approval→ To-Entity Approval→ Finance Review→ Awaiting Confirmation→ Completed

Both the charging and the charged entity must approve. If either declines (From-Entity Rejected / To-Entity Rejected) the chargeback stops.

You won't memorise these The Workflow Stepper translates every status into a plain label and highlights the current step — use it rather than the raw status codes.
Example Scenario A hiring request sits at Group CEO Approval for two days. The requester checks the Stepper, sees it's awaiting an executive sign-off (not their own action), and waits — the SLA is paused during approval.

Approval Workflow Guide

Purpose
Explain who approves what, and how to act on approvals.
Scope
All approval-bearing request types.
Audience
Managers, department approvers, executives, requesters.

5.1 Approval Matrix

Request TypeApproval chain (in order)Decision rule
Software InstallationIT triage → designated approverSingle approval to proceed.
New HardwareManager → CEO → CTO → CFO (payment)Each tier must approve in sequence; any rejection ends it.
New Hiring RequestCEO → Group CEO → Hiring Manager (candidate selection) → HR Screening → LOA approvalSequential; candidate rejection re-opens posting.
Purchase RequisitionFinance acknowledge → Finance Head / CFORouted by value/entity; approval required to pay.
Budget ProposalFinance review → Finance Head / CFOApproval required to adopt.
Inter-Company ChargebackFrom-Entity approver → To-Entity approver → Finance reviewBoth entities must approve; either rejection stops it.
How routing is decided Approvers are determined by your entity (business unit) and request type. Multi-entity items (like chargebacks) create one approval task per entity, and the request only advances when all required approvals are granted.

5.2 How to approve or reject (for managers/approvers)

  1. You're notified by email and in-app when something needs your decision.
  2. Open the Approval Center (/approvals) to see your queue.
  3. Select the item to review its details, justification and attachments.
  4. Choose Approve or Reject, and always add a comment — especially on rejection.
  5. The request advances to the next tier or returns to the requester automatically.

5.3 What requesters should know

  • While an item awaits approval, its SLA timer is paused — delays at the approval stage don't count against the SLA.
  • You'll be notified of each approval decision.
  • If rejected, read the approver's comment, correct the issue, and resubmit where the workflow allows.
Best Practices (approvers) Act within one business day where possible; an approval left idle beyond 14 days is auto-released to keep work flowing (see Business Rules).
Common Mistakes Rejecting without a comment leaves the requester unable to fix the problem. Always state why.
Troubleshooting Don't see an item you expect to approve? Confirm you hold the right role (e.g. Hiring Manager, Finance Head) and that the request is routed to your entity.

Knowledge Base User Guide

Purpose
Help users self-serve before raising a ticket.
Scope
Search, browse, read and rate articles.
Audience
All employees.

6.1 Finding answers

  1. Open the Knowledge Base (/kb).
  2. Type keywords in the search box — it matches article titles and content.
  3. Or browse by category (e.g. General) or filter by service desk (IT, HR, Finance).
  4. Select an article to open the Article Detail page with the full content, author and date.

6.2 Rating an article

At the bottom of each article, mark it Helpful or Not Helpful. Your vote improves article quality and helps surface the best answers. You can vote once per article.

Best Practices Search with the error message or a specific noun (“VPN”, “payslip”, “purchase requisition”) rather than a full sentence.
Common Mistakes Skipping the KB and raising a ticket for something with a published instant answer — slower for you and the desk.
Example Scenario Before raising an email ticket, Sam searches “Outlook signature”, finds the setup article, follows it, and marks it Helpful — no ticket needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Purpose
Answer the most common employee questions quickly.
Scope
Access, requests, status, approvals, notifications.
Audience
All employees.

Getting started

Q: How do I log in?
Use your company email and password on the Login screen. Reset via Forgot password if needed.

Q: Which desk do I use?
IT for technical/equipment, HR for people matters, Finance for purchases, chargebacks and budgets. See the Service Catalog.

Requests & tracking

Q: Where do I see all my requests?
On My Requests — filter by open/closed, search and sort.

Q: What does “Action Required” mean?
The team needs information from you. Open the ticket and respond; the SLA may be paused until you do.

Q: My ticket hasn't moved — why?
Check the Workflow Stepper. It's usually awaiting an approver's decision (SLA paused) or your own response.

Q: Can I re-open a resolved ticket?
Yes — add a comment explaining the issue persists.

Approvals

Q: Why does my hardware/finance request take longer?
These require multi-tier approval before work begins. Approval time doesn't count against the SLA.

Q: I'm a manager but can't see an approval.
Confirm your role and that the item is routed to your entity. See Document 9.

Notifications

Q: How am I notified?
Both in-app (real-time, shown in your Inbox) and by email. Email can be toggled globally by Admins.

Q: Where's my notification history?
The Unified Inbox (/inbox) — filter and mark items read.

Knowledge Base

Q: Do I need an account to read articles?
Articles are available to logged-in employees; search and browse freely.

Business Rules Guide

Purpose
Document the rules that govern SLAs, escalation and approvals.
Scope
SLA timers, pause/resume, escalation, approval triggers, role rules.
Audience
All employees, managers, approvers.

8.1 SLA Definitions

Each request type has a Service Level Agreement (SLA) measured in hours from submission. The deadline is shown on the request.

SLAApplies to
24 hoursGet IT Help, Email Management, Report System Problem, Question for HR
48 hoursSoftware Installation, New Hiring, Onboarding, Offboarding
72 hoursNew Hardware, Purchase Requisition, Inter-Company Chargeback, Budget Proposal

8.2 SLA Pause & Resume

  • The SLA timer pauses automatically when a request enters an approval stage (e.g. awaiting CEO/CFO/manager).
  • It resumes when the approval is decided, and the original time remaining is preserved (the deadline shifts by the paused duration).
  • A request that stays paused longer than 14 days is auto-resumed to prevent it stalling indefinitely.

8.3 Escalation Procedures

If a request breaches its SLA deadline, the system records a breach and notifies the assignee and managers. Escalation rules then fire a set number of hours after the breach, notifying progressively more senior roles.

SLA Breached→ +1h → notify Agent→ +4h → notify Agent + CEO→ +24h → notify CEO + Group CEO

Illustrative chain — actual trigger hours and target roles are configured per request type by administrators. Eligible escalation roles include Admin, Agent, HR, IT, Finance, CEO, VP and Group CEO.

8.4 Approval Trigger Rules

RuleEffect
Request type marked “requires approval”Routes to the configured approval chain before work proceeds (Software, Hardware, all Finance types).
Role-restricted request typeOnly users with the required role may raise it (e.g. New Hiring requires Hiring Manager).
Entity-based routingApprover is chosen from the requester's entity or a selected entity field.
Multi-entity requestOne approval per entity; advances only when all approve, stops if any rejects.
Troubleshooting If a ticket breaches SLA while genuinely waiting on you, respond promptly — escalations notify your management.

Role-Based User Guide

Purpose
Clarify what each role can do and is responsible for.
Scope
All end-user-facing roles.
Audience
All employees.

Your access is governed by your role(s). Most employees are Normal Staff. Additional roles unlock approval queues and specialised actions.

9.1 Role Capabilities

RoleCan doKey screens
Normal Staff (all employees)Create & track requests, search KB, read announcements.Dashboard My Requests KB
Hiring ManagerRaise hiring requests; select candidates during Manager Review.Create Request Approval Center
Manager / ApproverApprove or reject items routed to them.Approval Center
Finance HeadApprove finance/reimbursement requests.Approval Center
Executives (CEO, CTO, CFO, Group CEO)Approve high-tier items (hardware, hiring, finance) at their gate.Approval Center
Agent / IT AgentTriage and resolve tickets; manage IT assets.Agent Dashboard Assets
AdminSystem configuration, users, SLA & escalation rules, audit.Admin Settings Audit Trail

9.2 Role-Based Journeys

Employee (Normal Staff)

Dashboard → choose desk → Create Request → track in My Requests → respond to Action Required → confirm resolution.

Manager / Approver

Receive notification → Approval Center → review & decide with a comment → item advances.

Hiring Manager

Raise New Hiring Request → executive approvals → at Manager Review, select 1–3 candidates (each with required documents) → HR continues screening & LOA.

Agent

Agent Dashboard → pick up My/Unassigned tickets → progress through statuses → resolve within SLA.

Best Practices Hold only the roles you need. If you require an approval or specialised role, raise an HR/IT request to have it granted.
Troubleshooting Missing a screen or action you expect? It's almost always a role/permission matter — permissions are cached briefly, so a recently granted role can take a few minutes to take effect.
